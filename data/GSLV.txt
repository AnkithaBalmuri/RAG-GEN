GSLV

Introduction

The Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle (GSLV) is a launch vehicle developed by ISRO to place satellites into geostationary transfer orbit and other missions requiring higher payload capacity.

Development

The GSLV program was developed to reduce dependence on foreign launch services and strengthen India's space capabilities.

Configuration

GSLV consists of:

* Solid first stage
* Liquid strap-on boosters
* Liquid second stage
* Cryogenic upper stage

Cryogenic Engine

The cryogenic stage uses liquid hydrogen and liquid oxygen.

Advantages:

* Higher efficiency
* Increased payload capability
* Advanced propulsion technology

Applications

GSLV launches:

* Communication satellites
* Weather satellites
* Navigation satellites
* Scientific missions

Major Missions

* GSAT missions
* INSAT missions
* Chandrayaan-2

Importance

The GSLV program significantly improved India's independent launch capability.

Summary

GSLV is one of ISRO's most important launch vehicles and demonstrates India's expertise in advanced rocket technology.
