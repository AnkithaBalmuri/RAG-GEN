MANGALYAAN

Introduction

Mangalyaan, also known as the Mars Orbiter Mission (MOM), was India's first mission to Mars.

Launch

The mission was launched on 5 November 2013 using the PSLV-C25 launch vehicle.

Objectives

Goals included:

* Demonstrate interplanetary mission capability
* Study Mars atmosphere
* Analyze Martian surface features

Mars Arrival

Mangalyaan successfully entered Mars orbit on 24 September 2014.

India became:

* First Asian nation to reach Mars orbit
* First country to succeed on its first attempt

Scientific Instruments

Payloads studied:

* Atmospheric composition
* Methane presence
* Surface imaging
* Martian weather

Achievements

The mission demonstrated:

* Cost-effective engineering
* Deep-space communication
* Interplanetary navigation

Global Significance

Mangalyaan received worldwide recognition for achieving major objectives at a relatively low cost.

Summary

Mangalyaan remains one of ISRO's greatest achievements and established India as a leading spacefaring nation capable of interplanetary exploration.
