GAGANYAAN

Introduction

Gaganyaan is India's first human spaceflight program developed by ISRO.

Objectives

The mission aims to:

* Send Indian astronauts into space
* Demonstrate human spaceflight capability
* Develop advanced spacecraft technologies

Crew Module

The spacecraft includes:

* Crew module
* Service module
* Life support systems
* Emergency escape system

Astronaut Training

Indian astronauts receive training in:

* Spacecraft operations
* Emergency procedures
* Microgravity adaptation

Importance

Gaganyaan will make India one of the few countries capable of independent human spaceflight.

Scientific Benefits

* Human physiology studies
* Space technology development
* Future space station planning

Future Plans

The mission is expected to support:

* Indian space station development
* Lunar exploration
* Deep-space missions

Summary

Gaganyaan is a historic step for India's space program and will expand the nation's capabilities in human space exploration.
