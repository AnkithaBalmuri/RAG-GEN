If you're building an Astronomy/Space RAG chatbot, it's useful to have a few **foundational documents** that cover the entire field from beginner to advanced. Here is a roadmap of topics and concepts.

# 1. Foundations of Astronomy

## What is Astronomy?

Astronomy is the scientific study of:

* Stars
* Planets
* Moons
* Galaxies
* Black holes
* The universe as a whole

### Main Branches

* Observational Astronomy
* Theoretical Astronomy
* Astrophysics
* Cosmology
* Planetary Science
* Astrobiology

---

## The Scale of the Universe

Key units:

* Astronomical Unit (AU)
* Light-Year
* Parsec

Examples:

* Earth → Sun = 1 AU
* Sun → Proxima Centauri = 4.24 light-years
* Milky Way diameter ≈ 100,000 light-years

## Star Formation

Process:

Nebula → Protostar → Main Sequence Star

* Molecular clouds

## Stellar Evolution

Low-mass stars:

Main Sequence → Red Giant → Planetary Nebula → White Dwarf

Massive stars:

Main Sequence → Red Supergiant → Supernova → Neutron Star / Black Hole
