Introduction
Satellite technology involves the design, construction, launch, and operation of artificial satellites orbiting Earth or other celestial bodies.

History
The era of satellite technology began with the launch of Sputnik 1 Launch in 1957.

Since then, thousands of satellites have been launched for scientific, commercial, and military purposes.

Components of a Satellite
A satellite typically contains:

Structure
Supports all onboard systems.

Power System
Uses solar panels and batteries.

Communication System
Transmits and receives data.

Propulsion System
Controls orbital maneuvers.

Thermal Control System
Maintains operational temperatures.

Payload
Performs mission-specific tasks.

Types of Satellites
Communication Satellites
Enable television, internet, and telecommunications.

Weather Satellites
Monitor atmospheric conditions.

Navigation Satellites
Provide positioning services.

Earth Observation Satellites
Study Earth's environment.

Scientific Satellites
Conduct astronomical and scientific research.
