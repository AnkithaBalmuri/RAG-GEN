Introduction
Chandrayaan-2 was India's second lunar mission designed to explore the Moon's south polar region.

Mission Components
The mission consisted of:

Orbiter

Vikram Lander

Pragyan Rover

Launch
Launch Date: July 22, 2019

Launch Vehicle: GSLV Mk III

Scientific Goals
Map the lunar surface

Study mineral composition

Search for water ice

Analyze lunar exosphere

Orbiter Success
Although the lander experienced difficulties, the orbiter remains one of the most successful lunar orbiters.

The orbiter carries advanced instruments including:

Terrain Mapping Camera

Synthetic Aperture Radar

Imaging Infrared Spectrometer

Discoveries
The orbiter contributed to studies of:

Water molecules

Lunar topography

Surface chemistry

Polar regions

Lessons Learned
Data from Chandrayaan-2 directly helped engineers improve Chandrayaan-3.

Summary
Despite the landing setback, Chandrayaan-2 remains a highly successful scientific mission and continues to provide valuable lunar data.
