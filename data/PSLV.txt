PSLV

Introduction

The Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle (PSLV) is one of ISRO's most successful and reliable launch vehicles.

Development

PSLV was developed to place satellites into polar and sun-synchronous orbits.

Configuration

PSLV has four stages:

* First stage: Solid fuel
* Second stage: Liquid fuel
* Third stage: Solid fuel
* Fourth stage: Liquid fuel

Achievements

PSLV has launched:

* Earth observation satellites
* Navigation satellites
* Scientific spacecraft
* International satellites

Major Missions

* Chandrayaan-1
* Mars Orbiter Mission
* RISAT satellites
* Cartosat satellites

World Record

In 2017, PSLV launched 104 satellites in a single mission, setting a world record.

Importance

PSLV established India as a major provider of commercial satellite launch services.

Summary

PSLV remains one of the world's most reliable launch vehicles and a cornerstone of India's space program.
