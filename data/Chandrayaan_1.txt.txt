Introduction
Chandrayaan-1 was India's first lunar exploration mission and marked the beginning of India's planetary exploration program.

Launch
Launch Date: October 22, 2008

Launch Vehicle: PSLV-C11

Mission Objectives
Create a detailed lunar map

Analyze mineral composition

Search for water molecules

Test deep-space technologies

Scientific Payloads
The mission carried instruments from:

India

NASA

ESA

Bulgaria

Major Discovery
The mission's most famous achievement was discovering evidence of water molecules on the Moon.

This discovery transformed scientific understanding of the lunar environment.

Mapping Activities
The spacecraft mapped:

Lunar surface features

Mineral deposits

Topographical structures

Global Impact
The discovery of water significantly influenced future lunar missions worldwide.

Summary
Chandrayaan-1 established India as a major participant in planetary exploration and made one of the most important lunar discoveries of the modern era.
